Trip Overview

Immerse yourself in the exotic paradise of Bali, where ancient temples meet pristine beaches and lush jungles. This carefully crafted two-week journey balances relaxation with adventure, cultural experiences with natural wonders, and traditional Balinese hospitality with modern comforts.

From the spiritual heart of Ubud to the surf-friendly shores of Canggu and the dramatic cliff-top views of Uluwatu, this itinerary showcases the diverse landscapes and rich cultural tapestry that make Bali one of the world's most beloved destinations.

Key Experiences

Sacred Monkey Forest

🐒

Walk through this enchanting forest sanctuary home to over 700 Balinese long-tailed macaques. The forest contains ancient temples and is considered a sacred spiritual center by locals.

Best Time
Early morning (8-10am) or late afternoon (3-5pm)
Duration
1-2 hours
Cost
80,000 IDR (~$5 USD) per adult
Tips
Don't bring food or plastic bags. Remove sunglasses and dangling jewelry. Don't stare directly at monkeys as they may perceive it as a challenge.

Mount Batur Sunrise Hike

🌋

Embark on an early morning trek up an active volcano to witness one of Bali's most magical experiences - sunrise from the summit of Mount Batur, with panoramic views across Lake Batur and neighboring Mount Agung.

Best Time
Start at 2:30am to reach summit by sunrise (5:45-6:15am)
Duration
6-7 hours (2-3 hours up, 1.5-2 hours down, plus time at summit)
Cost
500,000-700,000 IDR (~$35-45 USD) per person
Tips
Wear layers (it's cold before sunrise), sturdy shoes, and bring a headlamp. The trek is moderate difficulty but achievable for most fitness levels.

Traditional Balinese Cooking Class

🍲

Learn the secrets of authentic Balinese cuisine in this hands-on culinary adventure. Visit local markets to select fresh ingredients, then prepare traditional dishes under the guidance of expert local chefs.

Best Time
Morning classes (8am-1pm) include market visits
Duration
4-6 hours
Cost
400,000-600,000 IDR (~$25-40 USD) per person
Booking
Reserve 1-2 days in advance, especially in high season

Practical Information

Transportation
  • Private drivers are the most comfortable option for longer journeys (400-600k IDR/day).
  • Ride-hailing apps (Gojek, Grab) work well in tourist areas but are banned in some locations.
  • Scooter rentals cost 60-80k IDR/day, but require international driving permit and experience.
Packing Essentials
  • Light, breathable clothing (cotton or linen)
  • Modest clothing for temple visits (shoulders and knees covered)
  • High SPF sunscreen, insect repellent, and rain jacket/umbrella
  • Portable water bottle with filter (tap water isn't potable)
Money Matters
  • ATMs widely available in tourist areas (daily withdrawal limit usually 2-3 million IDR)
  • Credit cards accepted at upscale establishments but carry cash for smaller vendors
  • Tipping not required but appreciated (10% for good service)
Connectivity
  • Local SIM cards available at airport (Telkomsel recommended for coverage)
  • Most accommodations and cafes offer free WiFi
  • Download Gojek, WhatsApp, and Maps.me for offline navigation
